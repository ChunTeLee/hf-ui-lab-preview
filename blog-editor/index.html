Before · shipping today

The current editor

A static replica of production /new-blog, captured Aug 2026: Edit/Preview tabs, always-on syntax rail, and every piece of metadata compressed into icon-only controls in the bottom bar.
After · Option 1

Editor + article settings panel

The URL moves to the top; thumbnail and authors live in a persistent right panel (iOS-style handle to collapse) with a big 1200×648 preview. Footer keeps the draft selector, last-saved status, delete, draft and publish.
Option 2 · Simplify archived

One quiet canvas

No Edit/Preview tabs — preview is a toggle on the editor itself. The syntax guide collapses to chips and all metadata tucks behind an “Article settings” popover in the footer.
